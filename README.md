# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behaviour Analysis

A comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project that examines **3,900 retail transactions** to uncover customer purchasing patterns, demographic trends, product performance, and actionable business insights. This project transforms raw transactional data into meaningful visualizations and strategic recommendations for data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Understanding customer shopping behavior is essential for improving marketing strategies, customer retention, and revenue growth. This project analyzes customer demographics, purchasing habits, subscription behavior, discount usage, and shipping preferences to identify key factors influencing business performance.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze customer purchase behavior
- Identify high-value customer segments
- Evaluate revenue contribution by demographics
- Discover top-performing products
- Assess the impact of discounts and subscriptions
- Analyze shipping preferences
- Generate actionable business recommendations

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 👥 Revenue by Gender

- Male customers contribute **67.7%** of total revenue.
- Female customers contribute **32.3%**.
- Opportunity exists to improve engagement among female shoppers through targeted marketing.

---

### 🎯 Customer Segmentation

| Segment | Percentage |
|---------|-----------:|
| Loyal Customers | 79.9% |
| Returning Customers | 18.0% |
| New Customers | 2.1% |

**Insight:**
Customer retention is the primary driver of business growth, with nearly 80% of customers classified as loyal.

---

### 🛍️ Top Performing Products

#### Best Sellers

- Clothing → Blouse
- Accessories → Jewelry
- Footwear → Sandals

#### Highest Rated Products

| Product | Rating |
|---------|-------:|
| Gloves | 3.86 |
| Sandals | 3.84 |
| Boots | 3.82 |

---

### 💳 Subscription Analysis

- Subscribers: **27%**
- Non-Subscribers: **73%**

Although subscribers and non-subscribers spend nearly the same per purchase, non-subscribers generate significantly higher overall revenue due to their larger customer base.

---

### 💸 Discount Strategy

Highest discounted products:

- Hat (50%)
- Sneakers (49%)
- Coat (49%)

Customers purchasing discounted products continue to spend above average, indicating that discounts effectively drive sales without significantly reducing customer value.

---

### 🚚 Shipping Behavior

| Shipping Type | Average Purchase |
|--------------|----------------:|
| Express | $60.48 |
| Standard | $58.46 |

Customers choosing express shipping generally place higher-value orders, making them an attractive segment for premium marketing initiatives.

---

### 👶 Revenue by Age Group

- Young Adults generate the highest revenue.
- Middle-aged customers contribute consistent spending.
- Older age groups offer opportunities for targeted re-engagement campaigns.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Strengthen customer retention programs.
- Increase marketing efforts targeting female shoppers.
- Promote top-rated products to drive repeat purchases.
- Convert non-subscribers into subscribers through personalized offers.
- Review profit margins on heavily discounted products.
- Reward premium customers with express shipping incentives.
- Develop age-specific marketing campaigns.
